# weird-mirror
## Final project: The Spiral

*A continuous movement through states of being.*

An interactive weird mirror built in TouchDesigner with webcam hand and body tracking (MediaPipe).
A black title screen fades into the mirror when someone steps in and moves. Behind them, 35
open-access artworks build out from the centre into a spiral, open into a rotating ring and gather
back in, moving through **birth, growth, death and rebirth** and returning to the Kongo cosmogram
it began with. Raise your hands and the artworks snap into a grid; point at one for about a second
and it comes forward with its title, date, artist and a short description, then returns to the
spiral after 8 seconds.
